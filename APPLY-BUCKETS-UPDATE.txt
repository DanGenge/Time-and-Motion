TIME & MOTION TRACKER — Buckets & Drag-to-Order Update
======================================================
Adds:
 • User-created BUCKETS (e.g. MMU, On-bench, Reload) to group activities
 • Vertical drag-to-reorder board (drag the ⠿ handle) — works on
   PC, tablet and iPhone (SortableJS, touch-enabled)
 • ↑ ↓ arrow buttons as a reliable fallback on every activity & bucket
 • Drag activities between buckets
 • Rename / delete / reorder buckets
 • Standard library now drops activities into sensible buckets
 • Tracker groups activities by your buckets, in your chosen order

====================================================================
STEP 1 — RUN THE MIGRATION (once)  ~15 seconds
====================================================================
1. Supabase Dashboard -> SQL Editor -> New query
2. Paste ALL of  sql/migration_v3.sql  -> Run
3. Expect "Success. No rows returned."  (Additive only — nothing deleted.)

====================================================================
STEP 2 — COPY FILES INTO YOUR CLONED REPO (GitHub Desktop)
====================================================================
Overwrite / add:

  ROOT (overwrite):
    templates.html
    tracker.html

  js\ (overwrite):
    activityLibrary.js

  sql\ (add):
    migration_v3.sql

DO NOT TOUCH (leave as-is):
    js\config.js  (your live keys)
    everything else you already have

====================================================================
STEP 3 — COMMIT & PUSH (GitHub Desktop)
====================================================================
  Summary: "Buckets + drag-to-order board"
  Commit to main -> Push origin  (Netlify redeploys ~30 sec)

====================================================================
STEP 4 — USE IT
====================================================================
Templates page:
 • "+ New bucket"  -> name it (MMU, On-bench, Reload…)
 • "+ New activity" -> pick its bucket + category
 • Drag the ⠿ handle on an activity to reorder, or drag it into
   another bucket. Or use the ▲ ▼ arrows.
 • Drag a bucket's ⠿ header handle (or ▲ ▼) to reorder whole buckets.
 • Rename a bucket by typing in its title; Delete moves its activities
   to "Unsorted" (nothing is lost).
 • "Load standard MMU library" creates buckets + activities for you.

Tracker page:
 • Activities now appear grouped by your buckets, in your order —
   your logical top-to-bottom shift sequence.

Notes:
 • Existing activities (from before) start life in "Unsorted" — just
   drag them into buckets.
 • Reports & Export are unchanged and still work (they group by the
   accountability category, which every activity still keeps).
