TIME & MOTION TRACKER — Editable & Custom Categories (Badges)
=============================================================
What this adds:
 • Edit the 4 built-in badges (Task / Customer / Orica / Other) —
   rename them and change their colours to whatever you like.
 • Create your OWN categories (e.g. Glencore, Dyno, Contractor) with
   a custom label + colour.
 • Each custom category "rolls up" under one of the 4 reporting groups
   (Task/Customer/Orica/Other) so Reports & the hrs/MMU/day charts keep
   working exactly as before — nothing in analytics breaks.
 • Badges show your labels/colours on the Tracker cards, the guided
   sequence, and the recent-activity feed.
 • Every recorded entry snapshots its badge, so old data keeps its look
   even if you later rename/recolour/delete a category.

====================================================================
STEP 1 — RUN THE MIGRATION (once)  ~15 seconds
====================================================================
1. Supabase Dashboard -> SQL Editor -> New query
2. Paste ALL of  sql/migration_v4.sql  -> Run
3. Expect "Success. No rows returned."  (Additive only.)

====================================================================
STEP 2 — COPY FILES INTO YOUR CLONED REPO (GitHub Desktop)
====================================================================

  ROOT (overwrite):
    templates.html

  js\ (ADD the new one, overwrite the other):
    categories.js       (NEW FILE — add this)

DO NOT TOUCH:

====================================================================
STEP 3 — COMMIT & PUSH (GitHub Desktop)
====================================================================
  Summary: "Editable + custom categories"
  Commit to main -> Push origin   (Netlify redeploys ~30 sec)

====================================================================
STEP 4 — USE IT
====================================================================
Templates page:
 • Click the "🎨 Categories" button (top of the page).
 • Recolour any category with its colour swatch; rename by typing.
 • The 4 built-ins can be edited but not deleted; their reporting
   group is locked (keeps Reports stable).
 • "+ Add category": type a label (e.g. Glencore), pick a colour, and
   choose which reporting group it rolls up under (usually Customer),
   then Add.
 • When adding/editing an activity, the "Category (badge)" dropdown now
   lists all your categories — pick any of them.

Tracker page:
 • Activity cards, the guided sequence header, and the feed all show
   your custom badge labels & colours.

Notes:
 • Existing activities keep working; they map to the matching built-in
   badge until you assign a custom one.
 • Reports still groups by the 4 reporting groups (a "Glencore" badge
   that rolls up under Customer counts toward Customer in the charts).
   Say the word if you'd like Reports to also break out by custom
   category label — easy follow-up.
