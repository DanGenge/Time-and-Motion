Activities, buckets & categories

Drag the ⠿ handle to reorder (top-to-bottom = your logical sequence) or use the ↑ ↓ arrows. Create buckets to group activities, and edit or create your own coloured categories (Customer, Glencore, Dyno…).

Badge categories

Rename or recolour any category. Create your own — each custom one rolls up under a reporting group (Task / Customer / Orica / Other) so Reports stays consistent. The 4 built-ins can be edited but not deleted.
